# Funding Computational Support

To ensure the long-term sustainability of high quality technical services in our Faculty, PIs applying for external research funding should request an appropriate contribution (typically 10%) under Pooled Staff Costs to support Shared Laboratory Technical Support and/or Shared Computational Support.

Full information and instructions for doing this in WorkTribe are available [here](https://liveuclac-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/ucbtgvi_ucl_ac_uk/IQBhuY24k4phQocY383PjOWpAXwTLJ_Jj35gDWVFMnN3o4o?email=james.d.gilbert%40ucl.ac.uk&wdExp=TEAMS-TREATMENT&web=1) (UCL login required).
